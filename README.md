# Forever Profession Trainer

Plan your profession leveling in WoW Forever. The addon reads your character’s current skill, shows what to craft, checks total cost, and gives you an Auctionator shopping list.

The default routes are based on [WoW Professions’ WoW Forever guides](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever). 
Click **wow-professions.com** in the planner to copy the guide link for the selected profession.

Supports Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, and Tailoring up to skill 300.

## Getting Started

1. Type `/fpt` (or `/foreverprofessiontrainer`) and choose from the six profession tabs on the right. You can preview any profession before learning it.
2. The guide defaults to skill **75**. Pick **150** or **300** to plan farther, or use the slider for a custom target. The plan starts at your current skill, or at 1 for a profession you haven’t learned.
3. Save any crafted items marked **Keep ×N** for later steps.


Drag the bottom-right corner to resize the planner. It remembers the size for your next session.

### While crafting

Follow the highlighted recipe and switch at the skill shown. Craft counts are estimates, and the planner updates them as you gain skill. If you hit your trained cap before reaching the target, the planner prompts you to train the next rank.

### Shop for materials

1. Click **Export shopping list** and press **Ctrl+C** to copy the selected text.
2. At the Auction House, open Auctionator’s **Shopping** tab, click **Import**, paste the text, and click **Import** again.

### Auctionator Integration

Forever Profession Trainer requires Auctionator.

**Buy materials** estimates the gold you’ll spend. **Vendor sales** estimates what an NPC vendor will pay for the extra crafts. **After vendor sales** combines those amounts. A minus sign means you spend gold and a plus sign means you gain gold. Training and vendor recipe fees are separate.

Keep crafts marked **Keep ×N** for later steps. Vendor the extras for immediate gold, or list them on the Auction House if you expect to earn more after fees. Auction House sale proceeds vary and are outside this estimate.

Cost estimates require Auctionator to have a cached price for every Auction House item in the list. If prices are missing, scan the Auction House with Auctionator, then reopen the planner. The leveling steps and shopping list work without price data. Craft counts can vary with skill gains.


The export covers Auction House items. Vendor supplies appear separately in the planner. Check what you already own before buying.
